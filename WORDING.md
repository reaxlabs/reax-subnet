When I pause a competition, each champion follows its title age: three days at its full champion entitlement, then a linear decrease to zero by day seven. After that, keep-alive stays. A five-day-old title starts the pause at half its entitlement; a title at least seven days old starts at keep-alive only. I announce a pause at least 24 hours in advance if it affects a title that old.

For engine champions, the normal engine-pot decay continues and the champion fraction is frozen at pause start. Generalist champions share 75% equally; each co-champion follows its own crowning time and needs its own award approval.

Former-champion places end at pause start. Their shares never move up to the champion. Every unpaid share is burned. Keep-alive stays, and entries can still be evaluated during a pause.
